About Barangay Sapao

Barangay Sapao is a coastal community in Guiuan, Eastern Samar, known for its warm people, strong community ties, and natural attractions. Its shoreline, local landmarks, and shared traditions make it a place worth visiting for travelers who want more than just a beach stop.

Community Development

Sapao continues to grow through the work of local officials, residents, and community organizations. From livelihood and public services to cultural events and tourism initiatives, the barangay remains focused on improving daily life while preserving its identity and heritage.

Contact the community
